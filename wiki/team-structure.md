# Team Structure

## Product Manager

The PM owns the combined context stack and is responsible for connecting the DX and Platform streams.

## Developer Experience Stream

Responsibilities:

- AI proficiency enablement
- AI tooling rollout
- Agent workflow training
- Developer communication and adoption measurement

Current interest:

- Turn AI adoption from tool access into practical delivery capability.

## Platform Engineering Stream

Responsibilities:

- Self-service workload setup
- Golden paths to production
- Internal platform reliability
- Runtime, observability, and ownership standards

Current interest:

- Validate self-service deployment before expanding to production workloads.

## Shared Operating Principle

The streams should not be managed as isolated roadmaps when one investment can advance both.
