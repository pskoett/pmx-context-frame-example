# OKRs

## Cycle

Q2 2026 example cycle.

## Objective 1: Prove AI adoption through a real agent-first developer workflow

Key results:

- 25 internal developers deploy at least one vibe-coded app through the guided platform path.
- 70 percent of pilot participants complete the flow without direct platform team support.
- AI adoption reporting includes workflow completion and deployment quality, not just tool usage.

## Objective 2: Validate the self-service workload platform before production expansion

Key results:

- 10 recurring setup issues are identified, ranked, and resolved or accepted.
- Every pilot app includes owner, runtime, repository, observability, and support metadata.
- The platform team can reuse at least 80 percent of the validated path for the next production workload class.

## Objective 3: Build an agent-readable platform context stack

Key results:

- Setup requirements are available as agent-readable markdown.
- The agent can generate a compliant deployment plan from app intent and repo context.
- Decision and assumption updates are captured weekly in this repo.
